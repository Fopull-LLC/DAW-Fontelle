Opening Fontelle on a Mac for the first time
============================================

Fontelle is free and is not signed with a paid Apple developer account, so
macOS asks you to confirm it once. After that it opens like any other app,
and it updates itself from its start menu without asking again.

1. Drag Fontelle.app into your Applications folder.
   (Opened from Downloads, macOS runs it from a temporary copy that cannot
   update itself.)

2. Open Fontelle from Applications. macOS says it cannot verify that
   "Fontelle" is free of malware. Press Done.

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security. Scroll down to Security,
   where it says "Fontelle" was blocked to protect your Mac, and press
   Open Anyway. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

4. Open Fontelle again and press Open Anyway once more. That is the last
   time.

On macOS 14 (Sonoma) and earlier, Control-click Fontelle.app in
Applications, choose Open, then Open.

If you are comfortable with Terminal, this does steps 2 to 4 in one go:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Fontelle.app

Recording asks for the microphone the first time you record; say Allow.
