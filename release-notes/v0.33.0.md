Clip colours that follow the mixer, soundfonts that are read correctly as instruments or kits, and the snap key working everywhere.

**Clips are the colour of their mixer track**

A clip is now always the colour of the mixer track it plays into, audio clips included. Before, a clip's own colour or a coloured lane could win, and audio clips stayed the default blue while the mixer outlined them in the track's colour. A clip's own colour still shows when it plays straight into the master. With a strip chosen on the mixer, the clips that don't feed it are dimmed.

**Soundfonts: instruments and kits**

Fontelle decides whether a soundfont preset is a drum kit or an instrument from the preset's name and its samples' names. Before, one narrow sample made the whole preset a kit, and a kit gets no scale or key, so the scale and key tools left out melodic presets sampled every key or two. In our test collection of 2,106 presets that was 267 of them. They now count as the instruments they are.

**The snap key**

G changes the grid snap in the arrangement too, not only in the piano roll. It works on whichever of the two you clicked in last, as Delete and S already do.
