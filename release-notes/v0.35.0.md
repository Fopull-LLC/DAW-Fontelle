Everything new since 0.34.0: Master, a mastering chain anyone can use; Tune can hear what key you're singing in; searchable menus; a full pass over Flopsynth's 409 presets; and smoother scrolling everywhere.

**Master: songs that sound finished, and loud enough**

A new effect for the end of your master track, with the steps a mastering engineer works through:

- **Clean**: cuts the rumble under what anyone can hear, and the top above it.
- **Tone**: four broad moves: low end, mud, presence and air.
- **Glue**: one knob of gentle bus compression to hold the mix together.
- **Space**: width, and the bass kept in the middle, so a mono club system and a phone both get all of it.
- **Speakers**: *Small speakers* lets phones and laptops hear the bass they can't play. *Check on* lets you hear your master as a mono system, a phone or a laptop would play it.
- **Loudness**: rides your song to a target loudness, the way streaming services measure it, under a ceiling it never goes past. This is what stops a Fontelle song sounding quieter than the next song in a playlist.

Ten presets get you most of the way: streaming, loud, gentle polish, warm, bright and airy, punchy, club, voice and podcast, lo-fi, and natural.

**Tune hears your key**

Press *Listen for key* over Tune's pitch display, sing a few phrases, and press it again. Tune works out the key and scale you were singing in and sets them.

**Search in every menu**

Type in the *New instrument*, *Change instrument* and *Add effect* menus, and Flopsynth's own effect menu, and they narrow as you type. Installed plugins are found here too, not only in the plugin list. Enter picks the first match. Spaces and punctuation don't matter, so "obx" finds OB-Xf.

**Flopsynth's presets, gone over**

Every one of the 409 presets was measured and the weak spots fixed:

- Sampled pianos, kits and hits no longer click when a note ends.
- 42 presets lost a hidden rumble that ate headroom.
- 48 bright presets now run oversampled, so their top end is clean instead of harsh.
- Vibrato and depth knobs that did nothing now work, across the strings, woodwinds and more.
- More strings, brass, winds and voices now have a vibrato that the mod wheel deepens.
- Plucks that swelled in now strike.

A few presets changed noticeably: Morph Drone, Flip Sync, Stairs Bass, Toy Piano, Clock Divide, Steel and Neuro. Tell us what you think.

**Play and pause from plugin windows**

Click a plugin window's title bar, or the strip along its top, and Space plays and pauses the song, wherever the pointer is. Click inside the plugin itself and it gets its keys back, so typing in a plugin still works. Linux for now.

**Scrolling**

- Settings, the guide and the shortcuts page glide with the wheel, and give a little at the top and bottom.
- Touchpads follow your fingers and coast when you lift them, in those pages, the arrangement and the piano roll.
- The channel list, the browser's lists and menus slide smoothly instead of jumping a row at a time.
- Page Up, Page Down, Home and End move the long pages.

**Smaller things**

- The piano roll's cut tool snaps to the grid, as the arrangement's does.
- Settings opens on the section and scroll where you left it, even after a restart. The guide does the same while Fontelle is open.
- *What's new* and *Support Fontelle* are always on the start menu.
- Effect windows open tall enough to show all their controls.
- Pages, menus and messages fade in instead of appearing in a single frame.
