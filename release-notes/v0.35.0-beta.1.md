A beta: smoother scrolling everywhere you read a list or a page, settings that open where you left them, and What's new always on the start menu. Please tell us how it feels, especially on a touchpad.

**Scrolling**

- **Pages glide and spring.** Settings, the guide and the shortcuts page move smoothly with the wheel instead of jumping. At the top or bottom they give a little and spring back.
- **Touchpads follow your fingers.** On those pages, the arrangement and the piano roll, the view moves exactly as far as your fingers do. Lift them mid-swipe and it keeps going, slowing to a stop. On the pages it stretches like a rubber band past either end.
- **Lists glide too.** The channel list, the browser's lists and menus move smoothly by the row instead of jumping.
- **Page Up, Page Down, Home and End** move the long pages: a screen at a time, or to either end.

**Remembering where you were**

- **Settings opens where you left it:** the same section, scrolled to the same place, even after you restart Fontelle.
- **The guide does the same** while Fontelle is open.

**Smaller things**

- **What's new** is now a link under the version on every start menu, not only after an update. It opens these notes.
- **Pages, menus and messages fade in** instead of appearing in a single frame, and messages fade out at the end of their time.
